# Twin-Fish Reign

Playable campaign for **Build with Manon Mani**. **Manon Mani** and **Masila Mani** are a paired identity — pearl/sea and temple/sovereign — and both names sit on every plate. You are **Maravarman Kulasekara Pandyan I** (Yuvaraja 1251–1268, reign 1268–1308/1310 CE), last great emperor of the classical Pandya empire at territorial zenith — until the 1308 plate, when Sundara’s patricide raises **Jatavarman Vira Pandya II**.

Open this plate from the home menu: **Campaign**. Skirmish stays the random-map 1v1. This chronicle is sequential, local, and original-engine — no Age of Empires names, no Microsoft assets. Historical Pandya people, ports, temples, and events are the ones in the source briefs.

Plates follow the MKP I timeline in `/docs/mkp1-timeline.md`. Academy, piracy, and Kayal infrastructure sit inside plates 1–2, not as a sixth pile.

---

## The emperor

Son of **Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan I** (1251–1268) — who crushed the Hoysalas, broke the Cholas, and made the Pandyas the South Indian superpower — and, in some registers, **Hemkatna Raja Lakshmi**. Trained in Madurai (Silambam, sword, horse, elephants, Tamil and Sanskrit). Appointed **Yuvaraja**; governed provinces and co-signed orders in his father’s shadow for nearly two decades.

Ended Chola rule in **1279 CE** (Rajendra III of the Cholas; Ramanatha of the Hoysalas) — the unfinished work, on plate 3. Expanded across South India, Andhra, Karnataka, Kerala; expedition to Sri Lanka. Maritime wealth at **Kayal of Manon Mani and Masila Mani**: pearl fisheries and horse trading. **1308 patricide** opened the **Pandyan Civil War (1308–1323)** and the road to **Malik Kafur**.

Marco Polo visited **1288** and **1293** as flavor on plate 4. Abdulla Wassaf wrote the commerce. Twin-fish gold and copper coinage. Korkai had silted; Kayal (Palayakayal, Tamraparani estuary) was the chief emporium.

Not an ascetic spiritual journey. Royal religious patronage, faction diplomacy, and devotional governance.

## Five plates, in timeline order

Sealed until the previous victory. One local chronicle on this device (`mm-mkp1-chronicle-t2`). Yuvaraja years are plate 1. Hard-refresh starts a blank Yuvaraja chronicle — old Kayal-first wax is not imported.

### 1. Konerinmaikondan — Yuvaraja Years (c. 1251–1268)

Co-regent with **Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan I** at **Kayal of Manon Mani and Masila Mani**.

1. **Konerinmaikondan / Nadu–Sabha** — Grain seals joint edicts. Prestige ≥ 50 bypasses assembly vetoes and speeds construction.
2. **Bank of Madurai** — Temple gold with **Aiyan Malavarayan**. Repay or morale cracks.
3. **Chaturvedimangalams and scriptoriums** — Spend Relics or Grain on university villages; Timber on palm-leaf scriptoriums. Prestige and edict speed. Stacks CSI if Sonakarpalli is already granted.
4. **Sonakarpalli** — 8th-regnal-year grants to Sonagars or a Chinese quay. Mosque auto-constructs.
5. **Southern grain / irrigation drought** — Drill the breadbasket (tanks or two caravans). Sets up Sri Lanka later.

- **Win:** Konerinmaikondan 50, one academy grant, mosque standing, drought survived.
- **Lose:** hearth, empty grain before tanks, or Nadu revolt (unrest 100).
- **Carry:** prestige, mosque, tanks/caravans, open temple books, veteran cadre.

### 2. Kayal Under Sail — Kayal Emporium (from 1268)

Supreme throne. Finish the father’s work at the port, not yet the island war.

1. **Tamraparani dredging** — Timber and Ore. Deep berths; horse hulls run faster.
2. **Manapad–Kulasekharapatnam lights** — Stone lighthouses cut galley ambush and night fog.
3. **Rowther Palayam** — Cavalry canton at Kulasekharapatnam. Crushes beach landings.
4. **Al-Tibi / Anjuvannam autonomy** — Seat **Takiuddin Abdur Rahman** as port governor. Unlocks Marzban mercenary escorts.
5. **Malabar galley ambushes** — Corsairs spawn on the horse-ships before they reach the gulf. Escorts, lights, Palayam, and a dredged mouth keep the road.

- **Win:** Al-Tibi seated, mouth dredged, Palayam or a Manapad light standing, three horse convoys at the quay.
- **Lose:** three convoys sink, or the Kayal hearth falls.
- **Mechanics:** Yuvaraja co-sign still calls veteran guards. Dual loyalty (Manigramam / Anjuvannam) remains a second path to escorts.

### 3. Storm of Subhagiri — Chola End and Island War (late 1270s)

1. **End the Chola line (1279)** — Rajendra III of the Cholas; Ramanatha of the Hoysalas. Ore and Grain close the father’s unfinished work.
2. **Aryachakravarti** lands across **Palk Strait** — Manon Mani’s crossing, Masila Mani’s war. Storm **Subhagiri (Yapahuwa)** and take the **Tooth Relic**.
3. Northern Sri Lanka as a tribute buffer so no fleet can invade Kayal via Palk — the keep/return choice waits for Parakramabahu III on plate 4.

- **Win:** Chola line closed, Tooth Relic deposited at the landing hearth.
- **Lose:** landing hearth razed before the relic is home.
- **No Relic Diplomacy overlay on this plate.**

### 4. Five Brother Kings — Relic Diplomacy (c. 1288–1293)

1. **Marco Polo** (1288 / 1293) is flavor in the estuary, not a mission win.
2. **Confederacy:** seat **Jatavarman Vira Pandyan I** (west), **Maravarman Vikkiraman III** (administration), **Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan II** (interior).
3. **Parakramabahu III** comes for the Tooth Relic **Aryachakravarti** took. **Keep** in Madurai or **return** so Jaffna stays a vassal.

Divine Patronage (Shaiva / Vaishnava) at Tirunelveli, Tharangambadi / **Masilamaninathar**, Madurai Meenakshi, Srirangam still spends Kayal customs gold.

- **Win:** confederacy average ≥ 50, then keep or return.
- **Lose:** estuary hearth falls before the choice is sealed.

### 5. Kayal Open to Kafur — The Fracture (1296–1323)

1. **1296:** **Jatavarman Vira Pandya II** named primary co-regent.
2. **1303:** **Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan III** given only a secondary co-regency.
3. **1308:** Sundara murders the emperor. Public and army raise Vira. Sundara flees to Delhi.
4. Civil war; **Malik Kafur** comes for **Kayal of Manon Mani and Masila Mani**.

A **Patricide** overlay pauses after March. **Take Vira’s banners**, then hold both hearths.

- **Win:** hold Vira’s Madurai and Kayal, raze Sundara’s camp, break two Kafur waves.
- **Lose:** Madurai or Kayal hearth falls after the patricide.
- Relic keep/return from plate 4 still scales later pirate pressure and tribute.

## How you open it

Home → **Campaign** → pick a chronicle plate → briefing → **March**.

Empty chronicles offer **Begin Konerinmaikondan**. Hard-refresh loads five timeline plates, Yuvaraja first. The **House of the Twin-Fish** profiles sit on the campaign table (father, mother, four brothers, two heirs, Aryachakravarti, Aiyan Malavarayan). A damaged seal can be recast. Victory unlocks the next plate. After the brother-kings plate, a **Relic Diplomacy** overlay asks keep or return. After March on the Fracture, a **Patricide** overlay raises Vira.

Match URL: `/match?source=campaign&mission=edicts` (then `kayal`, `palk`, `tamraparani`, `fracture`).

## States

- **Empty** — blank chronicle. Inscribe Konerinmaikondan (Yuvaraja first).
- **Briefing** — paused sim, historical beat, house line, win/lose lines, March.
- **Patricide** — 1308 overlay; Take Vira’s banners. Sim paused.
- **Diplomacy** — plate 4 only; Tooth Relic keep or return. Sim paused.
- **Victory** — pearl flourish, next plate unlocked.
- **Defeat** — hearth or objective failed, retry the same plate.
