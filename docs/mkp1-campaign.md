# Twin-Fish Reign

Playable campaign for **Build with Manon Mani**. **Manon Mani** and **Masila Mani** are a paired identity — pearl/sea and temple/sovereign — and both names sit on every plate. You are **Maravarman Kulasekara Pandyan I** (Yuvaraja 1251–1268, reign 1268–1308/1310 CE), last great emperor of the classical Pandya empire at territorial zenith — until the 1308 plate, when Sundara’s patricide raises **Jatavarman Vira Pandya II**.

Open this plate from the home menu: **Campaign**. Skirmish stays the random-map 1v1. This chronicle is sequential, local, and original-engine — no Age of Empires names, no Microsoft assets. Historical Pandya people, ports, temples, and events are the ones in the source briefs.

---

## The emperor

Son of **Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan I** (1251–1268) — who crushed the Hoysalas, broke the Cholas, and made the Pandyas the South Indian superpower — and, in some registers, **Hemkatna Raja Lakshmi**. Trained in Madurai (Silambam, sword, horse, elephants, Tamil and Sanskrit). Appointed **Yuvaraja**; governed provinces and co-signed orders in his father’s shadow for nearly two decades.

Ended Chola rule in **1279 CE** (Rajendra III of the Cholas; Ramanatha of the Hoysalas) — the unfinished work. Expanded across South India, Andhra, Karnataka, Kerala; expedition to Sri Lanka. Maritime wealth at **Kayal of Manon Mani and Masila Mani**: pearl fisheries and horse trading. **1308 patricide** opened the **Pandyan Civil War (1308–1323)** and the road to **Malik Kafur**.

Marco Polo visited **1288** and **1293**. Abdulla Wassaf wrote the commerce. Twin-fish gold and copper coinage. Korkai had silted; Kayal (Palayakayal, Tamraparani estuary) was the chief emporium.

Not an ascetic spiritual journey. Royal religious patronage, faction diplomacy, and devotional governance.

## Four plates, in order

Sealed until the previous victory. One local chronicle on this device.

### 1. Kayal Under Sail — Marzban Coalition

**1268s, the estuary of Manon Mani and Masila Mani. Yuvaraja no longer.** The father’s shadow still sits on Madurai. Appoint **Takiuddin Abdur Rahman** (Al-Tibi, Persian Gulf) as Prime Minister and port governor over Kayal, Kulasekharapatnam, Fattan, and Malifattan. Co-sign the old Yuvaraja orders for veteran banners.

Malabar corsairs cut the horse ships out of **Hormuz** and **Aden**. You hold the quay, fund the guilds, and get the warhorses ashore. The **Sonakarpalli** inscription: crown ministers sponsor a mosque for Arab settlers. That is the first beat of the **Cosmopolitan Stability Index**.

- **Win:** three horse convoys reach the Kayal quay.
- **Lose:** three convoys sink, or the Kayal hearth falls.
- **Mechanics:** **Yuvaraja / shadow of the father** — Grain co-signs orders, raises the Yuvaraja meter, and calls a veteran guard. Dual loyalty — **Manigramam** versus **Anjuvannam**. Fund both to unlock **Marzban escorts**. Blend **Pandya dhoni** with armed Arab merchant hulls. CSI starts here: fund the mosque, or trade stays thin.

### 2. Storm of Subhagiri — Relic Diplomacy

**Late 1270s, Palk Strait — Manon Mani’s crossing, Masila Mani’s war.** The five brother kings still share the house. **Bhuvanaikabahu I** and **Savakanmaindan** sit astride the strait. **Jaffna** watches. General **Kulasekara Cinkaiariyan Aryachakravarti** lands, storms **Subhagiri (Yapahuwa)**, and takes the **Sacred Tooth Relic of the Buddha** toward Madurai.

Capture first: the enemy host is debuffed while the relic is in Pandya hands. Then **choose**:

- **Keep** it in Madurai — passive combat buffs for Manon Mani and Masila Mani’s banners; Jaffna still watches.
- **Return** it when **Parakramabahu III** comes to court — northern Sri Lanka and Jaffna stay a vassal; tribute follows.

- **Win:** capture the Tooth Relic on Yapahuwa, deposit it, then keep or return.
- **Lose:** the landing hearth is razed before the choice is sealed.
- **Mechanics:** naval landing, cliff fortress, multi-stage relic quest. Keep or return both lower later pirate spawn; return stretches it further and pays tribute on later plates.

### 3. Lights on the Tamraparani — Divine Patronage

Marco Polo names **five brother kings**. **Confederacy System:** seat **Jatavarman Vira Pandyan I** (west), **Maravarman Vikkiraman III** (administration from ~1283), and **Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan II** (interior from 1277). Loyalty boosts stability; neglect stagnates those regions (unrest, Grain drip).

Stone lighthouses toward **Kulasekharapatnam** and **Manapad**. **Shark Charmers (Kadalkatti)** — Marco Polo’s note. **Rowther** cavalry on the sand.

Personal lean: **Shaivism**. Pragmatic funding of **Vaishnavism** for domain unity. Inscriptions from the 3rd–40th regnal year name **Tiruchendur**, **Srirangam**, **Tirukkadaiyur**. Spend Kayal customs gold (Relics) on temple upgrades:

- **Nellaiappar Temple, Tirunelveli** — grand outer walls
- **Masilamaninathar (Manivanneswaram), Tharangambadi** — **Masila Mani**, paired everywhere with **Manon Mani**
- **Madurai Meenakshi Temple** expansions
- **Ranganathaswamy Temple, Srirangam**

- **Win:** two lighthouses and one Kadalkatti shrine standing after three beach waves.
- **Lose:** Kayal hearth falls, or six pearl divers are taken by sharks.
- **Mechanics:** lighthouses, charmers, Rowther. **Divine Patronage** — Shaiva and Vaishnava meters; unrest falls, loyalty rises, localized combat buffs beside a standing temple.

### 4. The Fracture — two queens, patricide, civil war, Malik Kafur

Two queens, two sons. **1296:** **Jatavarman Vira Pandya II** (elder illegitimate son of a favored consort, the better commander) named primary co-regent. **1303:** **Jatavarman Sundara Pandyan III** (legitimate son of the primary queen) given only a secondary co-regency.

**1308:** Sundara murders the emperor. Public and army revolt and raise Vira. Sundara flees north to the Delhi Sultanate. **Malik Kafur** comes for **Kayal of Manon Mani and Masila Mani**.

A **Patricide** overlay pauses the sim after March. **Take Vira’s banners**, then hold both hearths.

The **Cosmopolitan Stability Index** is the coast’s weather. Confederacy seats still apply. Starve CSI and convoys starve. Push CSI without Shaiva/Vaishnava patronage and **traditionalist factions revolt**.

- **Win:** hold Vira’s Madurai and Kayal, raze Sundara’s camp, break two Kafur waves.
- **Lose:** Madurai or Kayal hearth falls after the patricide.
- **Mechanics:** patricide freeze → Vira banners; two hearths; Kafur from the north; CSI revolt; relic keep/return from Subhagiri still applies.

## How you open it

Home → **Campaign** → pick a chronicle plate → briefing → **March**.

Empty chronicles offer **Begin Kayal Under Sail**. The **House of the Twin-Fish** profiles sit on the campaign table (father, mother, four brothers, two heirs, Aryachakravarti). A damaged seal (corrupt save) can be recast. Victory unlocks the next plate. Defeat returns you to the same briefing. After Yapahuwa, a **Relic Diplomacy** overlay asks keep or return. After March on the Fracture, a **Patricide** overlay raises Vira. Desktop: two-column chapter cards plus profile grid. Mobile: stacked plates, full-width March.

Match URL: `/match?source=campaign&mission=kayal` (then `palk`, `tamraparani`, `fracture`).

## New mechanics (20 Hz sim, canvas blit)

All of this is extra state on the existing tick. Skirmish worlds never allocate it.

| Lever | What it does |
|---|---|
| Yuvaraja | Grain co-signs father’s orders. Raises Yuvaraja meter; veteran guard; combat buff at 50. |
| Confederacy System | Seat Vira I (Ore), Vikkiraman (Grain), Sundara II (Timber). Average ≥ 60 feeds CSI and Grain. Average < 28 stagnates (unrest, Grain drip). |
| Dual loyalty | Grain funds Manigramam; ore funds Anjuvannam. Both ≥ 50 unlocks Marzban escorts. |
| Horse convoys | Lightweight hulls. Slow if CSI is starved. |
| Dhoni / Marzban / Rowther / Corsair | Water/land speed. Rowther bonus on beaches. |
| Quay, lighthouse, Kadalkatti shrine | Hulls, night, divers. |
| Divine Patronage | Spend Relics on Shaiva or Vaishnava. Temples at Tirunelveli, Tharangambadi / Masilamaninathar, Madurai Meenakshi, Srirangam. Combat buff in temple radius. |
| Relic Diplomacy | Capture (enemy debuff) → keep (passive buffs) or return (Jaffna vassal + tribute). |
| Cosmopolitan Stability Index | Fund mosque, Buddhist shrine, and temples. Low CSI hurts trade. High CSI without patronage spawns traditionalist revolts. |
| Night / sharks / Kafur | Unchanged from the first slice. |
| Naming | **Manon Mani** and **Masila Mani** on Kayal, Madurai, Tharangambadi / Masilamaninathar, Tirunelveli, Srirangam, Tiruchendur, Tirukkadaiyur, Yapahuwa, Jaffna, Palk Strait. |

Renderer: CC0 campaign cards blit onto quay, hulls, Rowther, relic, pearls, lighthouses, and Kadalkatti shrines. Procedural fallback while a PNG loads. Atlas stays 8×4.

## States

- **Empty** — blank chronicle. Inscribe Kayal of Manon Mani and Masila Mani.
- **Briefing** — paused sim, historical beat, house line, win/lose lines, March.
- **Patricide** — 1308 overlay; Take Vira’s banners. Sim paused.
- **Diplomacy** — Tooth Relic captured; keep or return. Sim paused.
- **Victory** — pearl flourish, next plate unlocked, relic choice stored.
- **Defeat** — hearth or objective failed, retry the same plate.

Spell the general **Aryachakravarti**, never Aryacakravarti. **Manon Mani** (pearl/sea) is paired with **Masila Mani** (temple/sovereign) in game details, scripts, and every named place.
